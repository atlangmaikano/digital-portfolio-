COLLABORATION & DEVELOPMENT / 04

The value of working together.

Exploring how collaboration, communication and shared responsibility contribute to effective teamwork.

01 — INTRODUCTION

Different perspectives. Shared objectives.

Group work is an important part of academic development. It creates opportunities for students to exchange ideas, approach problems from different perspectives and learn how to work towards a common objective.

As a Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student, I recognise that collaboration is valuable in the professional world, where analysing information and solving organisational problems often requires people with different skills and perspectives.

02 — PRINCIPLES OF TEAMWORK

What effective collaboration requires.

01

Communication

Sharing ideas clearly, listening to others and ensuring that everyone understands the objectives.

02

Responsibility

Understanding individual responsibilities and contributing towards the completion of shared tasks.

03

Cooperation

Respecting different opinions and working together to find practical solutions.

04

Adaptability

Remaining open to different approaches and adjusting when a team encounters challenges.

03 — UNDERSTANDING TEAM DEVELOPMENT

Tuckman's five stages of group development.

The Tuckman model explains how groups can develop over time. It identifies five stages that help us understand the different dynamics involved in teamwork.

01

Forming

Members come together, get to know one another and begin understanding the group's objectives.

02

Storming

Differences in opinions, working styles and expectations may emerge as members share ideas.

03

Norming

Members establish common expectations, responsibilities and ways of working together.

04

Performing

The group works towards its objectives with greater coordination and shared understanding.

05

Adjourning

The group concludes its work, reviews its achievements and reflects on the experience.
