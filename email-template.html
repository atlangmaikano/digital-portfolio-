EMAIL COMMUNICATION

Making the first professional contact.

Professional communication is an important part of career development. The following email demonstrates how I would introduce myself and formally enquire about an industrial attachment opportunity.

New Message

To:

[Recipient's email address]

Subject:

Enquiry for Industrial Attachment Opportunity - Data Analytics

Dear Hiring Manager,

I hope this email finds you well.

My name is Atlang Maikano, and I am a first-year student pursuing Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana School of Business Science.

I am writing to enquire about the possibility of undertaking an industrial attachment at [Company Name]. I am particularly interested in gaining practical exposure to data analysis, database management and the use of data to support organisational decision-making.

I have a strong interest in numerical work and developing my analytical abilities. I would appreciate the opportunity to learn from professionals within your organisation and apply the knowledge I am acquiring through my studies.

I would be grateful if you could advise me on whether your organisation offers industrial attachment opportunities and the application requirements.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to your response.

Kind regards,

Atlang Maikano Business Intelligence and Data Analytics Student Botswana School of Business Science Phone: +267 75612606 Email: atlangmaikano632@gmail.com

PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE

Before sending an email.

01

Use a clear subject

Make the purpose of your email immediately understandable to the recipient.

02

Maintain professionalism

Use respectful language, proper grammar and a suitable greeting.

03

Include relevant documents

Attach your updated CV and application letter when requested or appropriate.

04

Review before sending

Check the recipient's address, spelling, contact details and attachments.
